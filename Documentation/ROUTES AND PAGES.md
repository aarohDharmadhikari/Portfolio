
# Routes and Pages

## Purpose

This document defines the responsibility of each portfolio page and establishes a consistent information architecture.

The target route structure is listed below. A route should only be considered implemented after its corresponding page has been created and verified.

| Route | Page | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Home | Introduction and selected portfolio highlights |
| `/about` | About Me | Background, interests, and professional direction |
| `/skills` | Skills | Verified technical capabilities |
| `/experience` | Experience | Relevant professional and project-based experience |
| `/projects` | Projects | Detailed presentation of selected projects |
| `/contact` | Contact | Contact methods and professional profiles |
| `/services` | Services | Future service information; under development until ready |

## Home

The homepage introduces the portfolio owner and establishes the website's visual identity.

It should prioritize:
- Name and professional designation.
- A concise introduction.
- Primary navigation or calls to action.
- Selected project highlights.
- A small preview of relevant skills.

The homepage should not become a long, overloaded collection of every portfolio section.

## About Me

Presents personal and academic background, technical interests, and professional direction.

Content must remain factual and should not repeat the entire project catalogue.

## Skills

Groups verified technologies and capabilities into understandable categories.

Do not add a technology solely because it is popular or appears in a project idea.

## Experience

Presents verified experience. Personal projects, collaborative work, internships, and employment must be distinguished accurately.

Do not invent employers, roles, responsibilities, or dates.

## Projects

Displays selected individual and collaborative work.

Each project should have an accurate description, relevant technologies, a verified status, and working links where available.

Collaborative work must identify my contribution.

## Contact

Provides verified contact methods, including email and professional profiles.

Interactive controls should provide clear feedback and remain accessible.

## Services

This page is reserved for potential future services. Until the page is ready, it must clearly communicate that it is under development.

Do not display unverified pricing, order workflows, or service commitments.

## Navigation Requirements

- Each primary navigation item should lead to its intended route.
- Active or current navigation states should be understandable.
- Mobile navigation should be usable with keyboard and touch input.
- Links must have meaningful labels.
- Navigation should not depend exclusively on scrolling to sections on the homepage.

## Verification

After implementation, verify every route directly, including refresh behavior, unknown routes, navigation links, and responsive layouts.
